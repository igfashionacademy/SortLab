Cookies

SortLab sets no cookies of its own. There are no analytics, advertising, social media or tracking cookies.

To remember your choices between visits, SortLab saves the following in your browser. Nothing here is sent to anyone.

Why there is no cookie banner

Under Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code (implementing Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive) and the Garante’s cookie guidelines of 10 June 2021, consent is not needed for storage that is strictly necessary to provide a service you have asked for. SortLab’s storage only remembers settings you choose and a photo you add, so no consent banner is shown. If tracking or analytics are ever added, they will stay off until you give consent.

How to delete stored data

  • Photo: open Customize and choose “Remove photo”.
  • Everything: clear this site’s data in your browser settings (often under Privacy → Site data). SortLab then goes back to its default settings.
