An educational visualisation tool

SortLab is meant for learning and for fun. It shows how sorting algorithms move data around. It is not a reference implementation, a benchmarking tool or professional software.

Animations are simplified

To be watchable, the animations record and replay individual steps such as comparisons and writes. Real implementations in programming languages and libraries often use extra optimisations, hybrid strategies or different memory layouts. Step counts, comparison counts and “write” counts follow SortLab’s own way of counting, which may differ from other sources.

Results are not benchmarks

Speed, smoothness and recording quality depend on your device, browser, settings and other open apps. Durations shown in SortLab are based on the chosen playback speed, not on real computing time. The winner of a Battle reflects step counts under SortLab’s settings, not a guaranteed performance ranking.
